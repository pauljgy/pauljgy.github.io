Matchups which I've had a bit more experience with:

- Teemo
	- Suprisingly hard, if the teemo can space/kite.
	- Locking him down is difficult because of his speed
	- Infinite shroom slows in tf
	- cant proc big autos because of the blind
- Jax/Olaf
	- Easily losable in the first few levels
	- Need to play extra safe and never let him get his burst
- Irelia
	- Extremely hard after she gets bork
- Yone/Riven
	- Extreme respect for level 6 spike
