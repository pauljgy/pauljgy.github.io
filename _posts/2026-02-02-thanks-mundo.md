Played some more Mundo today, and honestly his impact in the game feels a lot more tangible than other toplaners in the mid-late game.
If I can actually make it to mid-late without being too far behind, his mobility and damage let me run in literally a straight line to their carries and chunk them with heartsteel, while everyone on their team throws every ability they can at me.
I can more directly see (1) the threat I pose to the enemy carries and (2) the pressure I draw away from my backline, allowing them to dish out damage.

He also seems like a better blind pick, since if I'm not an idiot I can get through most lanes deathless maybe 10-20 cs behind. If I can get that far and my team hasn't lost yet, then the game is in a really good spot.

Now, the pool is looking like:

- Blinds: Mundo, Cho
- Counterpicks: Gragas, Jax, Garen/Darius
- Bans: Gwen (for Mundo), Voli, Ambessa? Malphite?
