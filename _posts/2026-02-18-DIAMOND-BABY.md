DIAMOND TOP LANER (W/L was 166-138, win rate of ~55%.)

W/L through emerald was 57/40.

# Lessons

## Draft
- Comp definitely matters
- Having mundo, jax, gragas covered enough playstyles to fit most comps

## Early game
- Laning needs to be extremely consistent
- Need to know matchups and what the enemy is looking for
- 8 cs/min minimum
- Almost never tp away and prioritize waves

## Mid game
- Need to apply as much pressure as possible on sidelane so you can create numbers advantages in midgame fights
- Best to save tp to help team, even if it means losing a plate or two

## Late game
- If the game gets this late, then draft is what decides the game
- Need to be extra skeptical about where you don't have vision/enemies that might be waiting to make a pick

## Meta
- Take a break between games
- Try your best to clearly define what value you add to the team, and how to get there
- Mute all
- Never ff
