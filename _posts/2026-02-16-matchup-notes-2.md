Matchups which I've had a bit more experience with:

- Pretty hard matchups
- Kayle
	- Hard to punish a decent kayle who can farm well
	- They will outscale unfortunately so pray the team can carry
	- Worth a ban alongside gwen
- Yorick
	- Ghouls make it hard to farm with q
	- Maybe take flash to avoid cage deaths in early-game?
	- He also can withstand Mundo push for a long time, but eventually mundo will outscale
