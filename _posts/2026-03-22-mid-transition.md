Many of the skills from top lane carried over, like:
- Trading fundamentals
- Sidelaning and drawing pressure
- Creating numbers advantages for fights

Some new thoughts:

1) Good cs is possibly more important here than top. My take now: losing some waves top leaves you way worse off for the 1v1, but since the 1v1 doesn't always decide the game, losing some cs won't damage your win probability too much. But being behind in xp/gold mid feels way worse for the whole game; the role just has a lot of influence on how the early/mid game goes, and if you aren't able to threaten much in this period because of bad cs, I think you end up being a huge liability.

2) Mechanics also need to be on point. Champs are less durable/more damage output, and a lot of the fights happen much faster than toplane.

3) Even more important to play around objectives, and not just for your lane. This means setting up back timers + spending gold before objectives, and being willing to trade hp/mana for prio.
