- Tanks
	- Generally need to respect high base dmg on tanks
- Ranged
	- Can win if cleavers are hitting, but otherwise ask for jungle help
- Fighters
	- Pretty easy, more predictable laning phase
- Scaling top laners
	- Yone, hard
	- Kayle, also kinda hard
	- Nasus, also hard
	- Vayne, hard but can be easy with jungle help
